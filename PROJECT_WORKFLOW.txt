1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SavidhanSamraksha (CivicShield) is an AI-powered, multi-tier governance and
monitoring platform developed for the Members of Parliament Local Area
Development Scheme (MPLADS) in India (covering 543 Lok Sabha and 250 Rajya Sabha
constituencies, disbursing ₹5 Crore per MP annually).

The platform solves the critical governance challenge of tracking physical
vs. financial progress, preventing fund misappropriation, detecting "ghost"
infrastructure projects, and establishing cryptographic accountability through:
1. Multi-variable Financial and Temporal Risk Analysis
2. Unsupervised Machine Learning Outlier Scoring (Isolation Forest)
3. Computer Vision Field Photo Forensic Auditing (EXIF & Perceptual Hashing)
4. Multi-Spectral Satellite Earth Observation (ESA Sentinel-2 via Google Earth Engine)
5. Tamper-evident Administrative State Machine & Audit Chaining

2. END-TO-END SYSTEM WORKFLOW
================================================================================

The platform operates across 6 integrated workflow phases:

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PHASE 1: DATA INGESTION & PIPELINE ETL                                       |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  • Official MPLADS administrative records are ingested via automated ETL
    pipelines (project ID, constituency, implementing agency, sanction date,
    cost, funds released, funds spent, reported physical progress %).
  • Data normalizers clean inconsistent dates, sanitize financial amounts,
    and geocode project sites to latitude/longitude coordinates.
  • Stored securely in PostgreSQL / Neon Cloud Serverless Database.

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PHASE 2: MULTI-TIER RISK & MACHINE LEARNING INFERENCE                        |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  • Every project is automatically evaluated by the ML Risk Engine across three
    complementary scoring layers:
    1. Financial-Temporal Module: Detects disbursement velocity mismatches
       (e.g., 85% funds spent while physical completion is only 15%).
    2. Administrative Audit Module: Flags missing Utilisation Certificates (UC),
       tender delays (>90 days), and completion date overruns.
    3. Isolation Forest ML Outlier Module: Runs scikit-learn Isolation Forest
       across regional peer projects (same category, state, budget scale)
       to pinpoint statistical anomalies invisible to standard rule checks.
  • Outputs a normalized Risk Score (0 to 100) and assigns a Risk Tier:
    LOW (0-29), MODERATE (30-59), HIGH (60-84), CRITICAL (85-100).

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PHASE 3: FIELD PHOTO INTELLIGENCE & COMPUTER VISION AUDITING                 |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  • When field inspectors or citizens submit milestone photographs:
    1. Perceptual Hashing (pHash): Computes a 64-bit fingerprint of each image.
       Compares Hamming distances to detect reused stock photos across
       different projects or duplicate progress claims within the same work.
    2. EXIF Metadata Forensic Parser: Inspects hardware tags, original camera
       timestamps, and embedded GPS tags. Stripped or tampered EXIF is flagged.
    3. Geospatial Geofence Verification: Computes spatial distance between photo
       GPS coordinates and the project centroid. Images taken outside the
       permitted perimeter (>15 km) trigger a Geofence Mismatch Anomaly.

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PHASE 4: SATELLITE EARTH OBSERVATION & CHANGE DETECTION                      |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  • Analyzes Copernicus Sentinel-2 10m multi-spectral optical satellite imagery
    queried via Google Earth Engine (GEE):
    1. Dual-Temporal Windowing: Acquires pre-construction baseline imagery (T0)
       at sanction date and current progress imagery (T1).
    2. Spectral Index Computation:
       - NDBI (Normalized Difference Built-up Index): Measures structural
         concrete, masonry, and roofing changes.
       - NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index): Measures land clearing.
    3. 100m Point-Radius Zonal Reduction: Averages reflectance over the site.
    4. Cloud Cover Mitigation: Automatically filters cloud occlusion using QA60/SCL
       masks. If overcast (>70%), re-normalizes risk weights without penalty.
    5. Forensic Ground Classification:
       - GHOST PROJECT: Funds spent or high progress reported, but near-zero
         satellite ground transformation detected (ΔNDBI < +0.02).
       - UNAUTHORIZED CONSTRUCTION: Heavy ground construction detected on site
         with zero administrative clearance or reports (ΔNDBI > +0.18).
       - VERIFIED ACTIVE CONSTRUCTION: Physical progress confirmed by satellite.

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PHASE 5: INTERACTIVE DASHBOARD & INVESTIGATIVE RADAR HUD                     |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  • State & District Dashboards: Visual heatmaps, sector breakdown charts,
    and priority escalation queues for District Collectors and CAG Auditors.
  • Project Deep-Dive View:
    - Side-by-side Dual-Date Satellite Radar Canvas (T0 vs. T1 lenses).
    - Interactive "Run Live Sentinel-2 Scan" button triggering an animated
      orbital acquisition HUD with radar sweep cone, telemetry progress,
      and dynamic "LIVE SCAN VERIFIED" certification badge.
    - Plain-English Forensic Explanations for all non-technical stakeholders.

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PHASE 6: ESCALATION STATE MACHINE & CRYPTOGRAPHIC AUDIT TRAIL                |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  • Automated State Machine: Transitions projects through formal governance
    states: NORMAL -> FLAGGED -> UNDER_REVIEW -> ESCALATED -> SANCTION_FROZEN.
  • SLA Enforcement: District Collectors are notified with hard review deadlines.
  • Cryptographic Audit Chain: Every administrative action, inquiry, or sanction
    freeze is stamped with a SHA-256 hash chained to the previous entry,
    ensuring tamper-proof accountability.

3. COMPLETE TECHNOLOGY STACK
================================================================================

A. FRONTEND USER INTERFACE & VISUALIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • React 19 (Modern Single Page Application Architecture)
  • Vite 7 (Ultra-fast HMR and production bundle optimization)
  • Tailwind CSS (v4 governance dark-mode styling and layout system)
  • Lucide React (Clean, accessible governance and telemetry icons)
  • HTML5 SVG Canvas Engines:
    - Custom Dual-Date Satellite Observation Lens Engine (T0 vs. T1)
    - Animated Space Radar HUD with concentric sweep beams & telemetry ticker
    - Ground change crosshair reticles & spectral delta readouts
  • Responsive UI: Designed for desktop command centers, tablets, & mobile.

B. BACKEND API & DATA SERVICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Python 3.11+ / FastAPI: High-throughput asynchronous REST API server
  • Uvicorn: ASGI high-concurrency production server
  • Pydantic v2: Strict request/response data validation and schema enforcement
  • Node.js & TypeScript (backend-case): Escalation router & state machine
  • psycopg2-binary: Resilient pooled PostgreSQL connection management
  • Python-Multipart: Secure multipart file uploads for field evidence

C. MACHINE LEARNING & RISK INTELLIGENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Scikit-Learn:
    - Isolation Forest (Unsupervised statistical anomaly & outlier detection)
    - MinMax & Robust Scalers for peer-group metric normalization
  • NumPy & Pandas: High-speed tabular feature engineering & mathematical deltas
  • Joblib: Model serialization and caching
  • Hybrid Inference: Domain heuristic rules + ML peer anomaly scoring

D. COMPUTER VISION & PHOTO FORENSICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Pillow (PIL): Image decoding, resizing, and color space transformations
  • ImageHash: Perceptual hashing (pHash) for Hamming distance fraud checks
  • ExifRead / Native EXIF Parser: Extracts camera hardware, ISO, timestamps,
    and embedded GPS coordinates
  • Shapely: Spatial geometry operations and geofence boundary checks

E. SATELLITE REMOTE SENSING & EARTH OBSERVATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Google Earth Engine API (earthengine-api): Programmatic access to ESA
    Copernicus Sentinel-2 Level-2A Multi-Spectral BOA Surface Reflectance tiles
  • Multi-Spectral Bands Used:
    - Band 4 (Red, 665 nm)
    - Band 8 (Near-Infrared / NIR, 842 nm)
    - Band 11 (Shortwave-Infrared / SWIR, 1610 nm)
  • Spectral Formulas:
    - NDBI = (SWIR - NIR) / (SWIR + NIR)  [Built-up structural alteration]
    - NDVI = (NIR - Red) / (NIR + Red)    [Vegetation / ground clearing]
  • Cloud Occlusion Filters: Sentinel-2 QA60 cloud bitmask & SCL scene classification
  • Dual-Mode Architecture:
    - High-Speed Mock Mode: Sub-50ms deterministic response for live presentations
    - Live GEE Mode: Live orbital queries with cloud-native credential loading

F. DATABASE & PERSISTENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • PostgreSQL 15+ / Neon Cloud Serverless Database
  • Drizzle ORM & Raw SQL Optimizations for complex aggregation queries
  • Relational Schema: Projects, Disbursals, Evidence Photos, Audit Logs, Users
  • Cryptographic Ledger: SHA-256 hash-chained immutable audit trail

G. CLOUD, DEVOPS & SECURITY INFRASTRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Render: Hosting FastAPI & ML Inference services with automatic CI/CD
  • Vercel: High-speed edge CDN deployment for the React SPA
  • Cloud-Native Secret Management: Zero .env files tracked in Git; direct
    environment variable loading (GEE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON) & /etc/secrets/
  • Docker & Docker Compose: Multi-container orchestration (API, DB, Frontend)
  • GitHub Actions: Continuous Integration test pipeline (8/8 Satellite,
    7/7 Isolation Forest, 10/10 Visual Engine tests passing at 100%)
